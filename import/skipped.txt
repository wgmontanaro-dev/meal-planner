Notes on parsing the Apple Notes dump (2026-08-28)
==================================================

~90 lines of notes -> 82 recipe rows in recipes.json.

MERGED (appeared more than once, or a sub-note of another recipe):
- "Cherry tomato risotto" appeared twice (mobkitchen.co.uk + a reddit thread)
  -> one row, reddit link kept in the summary.
- "Bratwurst" appeared twice ("Barbecue: bratwurst" and "Bratwurst from
  sausageman.co.uk" under To try) -> one row.
- "Ramsay's mashed potato with egg yolk and Parmesan" -> folded into the
  Shepherd's / cottage pie row (it's the topping in that note).

LOW CONFIDENCE / BORDERLINE (kept, but check or delete if unwanted):
- "Velveting chicken (marinade)" - a technique, not a meal. Has a clear
  quantity list so it was kept as a row.
- "Pan-fried then oven-baked chicken breast in butter" - technique note.
- "Sauteed potatoes with garlic" - side, technique note.
- "Lemon and herb chicken wings" (To try) - may duplicate the Nando's lemon
  & herb drumsticks row.

DROPPED:
- Image placeholders in the notes (screenshots) - not recoverable from
  plain text.

EVERY category field is a guess. cuisine is inferred from the title;
prepTimeCategory / storageType / dietType are defaults (mostly
FROM_30_TO_60 / MIXED, dietType guessed from the title). Review recipes.json
before running the importer with --apply.
